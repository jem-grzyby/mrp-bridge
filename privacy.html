MRP Bridge privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

  • Who uses it. MRP Bridge is a personal tool used only by its owner, signed in with the owner's own Google account.
  • What it reads. It asks for read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) and Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It does not send, delete or change email or calendar events.
  • Where data goes. Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server run by the app, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.
  • Limited use. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local data.
